The record

A record is one HTML page. Its head carries mplpb:document-id, mplpb:scope, mplpb:status, mplpb:hash, mplpb:derived-from and mplpb:origin-depth. Status is current or retired. The hash covers the title, the body and every field except status and the hash itself, so retiring a page changes one line and nothing the page says.

Optional fields: when-to-use, supersedes, origin, ratified-by, owner, updated, kind and points-to. Underscores and hyphens in field names are read as the same.
