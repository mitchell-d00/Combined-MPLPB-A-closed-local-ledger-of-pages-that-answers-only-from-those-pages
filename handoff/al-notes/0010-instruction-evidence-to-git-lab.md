## Question and graph choice

Can one bounded request retain a checked relationship and its missing date, then record that attributed assertion through a separately authorized Git effect? Can the same experiment distinguish an interpretation improvement from merely connecting two existing components?

| Surface | Use in this study |
| --- | --- |
| Chat language and typed conversation graphs | Phrase routing, declared context and candidate intents. Leave routing out of the effect path. |
| Reconstruction candidate graph | Reuse the deterministic `mplpb_work.prose_evidence.reconstruct_report` candidate information map and relationship graph as a read-only view. Log proposed role and relationship edges and their coordinates. Freeze whether there is any read-only candidate proposal comparison. |
| Bounded native source proof | The existing `relation_graph` verifies its own finite direct relation families. Source-attributed instructions belong to Strict Ask through `answer_query` and the existing `source_attribution.py`, `attributed_instruction.py` and documentary verifier paths. Use the exact original question and profile on current eligible bytes. A candidate edge supplies no proof or authority. |
| Gremlin | Find the code, dependencies and retained evaluations. Use it for change review and evidence navigation. |

The existing instruction contract explicitly emits writer and attributed instructor, but its two-role chain does not by itself prove a third embedded reporter or an instruction date. Use the native composition path for independently checked obligations only where it preserves the whole original request and verifies the relationship between roles. Do not infer a missing role or connect answers by matching names. Before extending Heka, establish a native positive for the exact frozen shapes on the unchanged baseline or report the blocker. Keep unsupported distinct-reporter/date shapes as withheld or deferred under their original frozen expectations.

Score candidate structure separately from native support. Keep writer/byline, embedded reporter, instructing actor, recipient, instruction, polarity at each asserted layer, conditions and the requested date role as separate obligations. Two mentions do not establish shared identity or a connecting relationship. Native citations establish correspondence to eligible bytes and revision. Native proof establishes support under the declared rules. [Chat routing] · [Candidate reconstruction] · [Finite proof graph] · [Documentary contracts] · [Instruction evaluation] · [Gremlin]

## Smallest useful slice

Use an invented archive and request such as “Record who instructed whom according to this report, and leave the instruction date unresolved if the source does not establish it.” Freeze one intended action, `record-canary`, to publish a source-attributed archival entry in a disposable repository. Keep the documentary question intact. Freeze the mandatory proof obligations for recording and the one date role allowed to remain unresolved. A native partial remains partial. Only its actually checked instruction claim may be projected into a record that names the missing date obligation. The host must explicitly authorize this recording operation. Text that records an instruction supplies no authority to execute it.

**Reuse Heka's effect boundary.** Heka's bridge already binds reader pin, exact question and obligation, a proof digest, a conservative whole-ledger snapshot digest, action, ref, expected and intended object IDs and reservation namespace. It checks proof again before final host admission. Its verifier is deliberately limited to a single affirmative, unqualified reported-authorship claim with exact named selection and witnesses. Reuse the Strict reader, snapshot/recheck protocol, controller, fixed CAS and reservation code. Add only a lab-local instruction-contract projection and host binding adapter, with a fixture/readback extension for a single canonical record file. Keep the original Heka apparatus intact. If a native obligation cannot be verified, the adapter withholds and records the gap. [Heka contract] · [Heka bridge] · [Controller policy] · [Reservation policy]

**Bind the actual effect.** Prebuild the intended Git object during fixture setup. Pin its exact canonical record bytes/digest, checked source IDs and revision/eligibility dependencies, reader and proof digest, the host-selected repository identity and path, ref, old/new OIDs, reservation namespace and applicable policy version/digest. The record carries its source-assertion status, explicit date status and source references. The current controller grant covers action and revision. Have the lab host supply and recheck a grant scoped to the exact operation binding before exposing the existing controller grant. Keep the one-resource namespace host-owned. The writer remains the same fixed CAS over explicit old/new OIDs. Independently inspect the target ref, marker and record digest, then check the durable receipt separately. Absent or changed required proof, policy, binding or grant blocks attempts. The source/host rechecks and Git CAS remain separate operations. Preexisting Apollo Ed25519 and Ares revocation results have separate scopes. [Apollo contract] · [Ares contract]

**Preservation and deferred composition.** Use the existing instruction and documentary controls for negation, conditional scope and ambiguous identity. Preserve Heka's source-update, competing CAS, failed/known duplicate and interruption cases, Hermes-02 namespace controls, Apollo receipt provenance, Ares pre-/post-decision revocation and restart rereads, and the controller/restart packets as consumed evidence. ## Acceptance and limits

Report separate exact fields and denominators for candidate interpretation, native claim support, citation validity, completeness and uncertainty, synthetic-oracle comparison, initial and final admission, attempts, observed OID/marker/payload, and durable completion. For each passing draw require three correct intended updates and receipts, zero attempts and no ref/marker/reservation changes in five blocked cases, and no unsupported edges promoted or missing dates filled. Retain all deviations, first outputs and scores. Keep scorer and held oracle outside the worker and public Git. Only a separately supported, authorized policy could record an accountable challenge as contested or promote a claim to a stronger status. This first slice records attributed source assertions and never promotes them to established facts.

The hypothesis can fail if a useful proposed relationship cannot survive native proof, if a missing obligation disappears, if a stale or misbound case attempts a write, if the exact record differs from the admitted payload, or if readback and durable completion are collapsed. A passing result demonstrates the frozen local path and its controls. It cannot establish unrestricted inference, broad language improvement, production readiness, trusted identity, a cross-resource transaction or independent historical adjudication. The oracle measures the authored fictional universe only. Preserve the existing historical-review protocol, reviewer scope and `NOT_RUN` labels. [Historical truth rules]
