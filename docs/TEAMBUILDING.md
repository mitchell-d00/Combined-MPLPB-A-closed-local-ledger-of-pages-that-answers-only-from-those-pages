# TeamBuilding

Last reviewed: 2026-11-26.

Package role: [research method and reproducible evidence](PACKAGE_GUIDE.md#evidence-and-limits).

Betty and Al name work steps in the TeamBuilding pipeline. Use your normal
assistant capabilities and follow the maintainer’s direction. Each step
operates with the tools and permissions available to that session.

## Shared purpose

Improve MPLPB’s handling of broader language through shared rules that preserve
valid behavior and enforce declared boundaries.

Keep three questions separate:

1. What does the source assert?
2. What may the system admit under its declared rules?
3. Is the assertion true?

MPLPB enforces its admission boundaries. Truth adjudication requires a separately
defined evaluation. Connections can suggest an investigation; they do not
automatically transfer evidence, permission or authority.

## Shared-rule criterion for Betty, Al and Outside

Build fewer shared rules that cover more classes of errors while preserving valid
behavior. Ask whether one maintained rule can replace several special handlers,
preserve their valid outcomes and handle fresh contrasting cases. Coverage gains
and consolidation are separate measurements; more passing cases alone do not
establish simplification.

1. Identify the common cause across affected paths.
2. Put the common boundary rule in one maintained place.
3. Keep specialized interpretation and proof rules where needed.
4. Test positive recovery and contrasting refusals across every affected path.
5. Remove redundant checks only after their responsibilities are covered.
6. Freeze the candidate, then measure fresh transfer.

Claim simplification when duplicated logic is reduced, valid outcomes survive and
fresh contrasts hold. Shared interpretation of entities, relations, negation and
qualifications still requires unchanged source evidence, eligibility, provenance
and admission boundaries. Literal claims and opening-time fields, for example,
must respect the same quotation/reporting-layer boundary. Preserve rejected
candidates and newly unsupported completions even when older contracts pass.

All three roles use this criterion through the existing manual handoff. Outside
remains Mitchell's sounding board; implementation direction comes from Mitchell.
This standing criterion adds no separate assignment or automatic test run.

## Broader coverage with preserved constraints

Canonical definition: **coverage-with-preserved-constraints-v1**.
Source: Mitchell D. McPhetridge, *Φ Under Pressure (PhiUP)*, §§2,8,18.1;
*Executable Supplement for The Recursive Machinery*, §2.

ΦUP distinguishes the objective (what improves) from the invariant (what must
survive). Its proposed persistence condition is `ΔH_gen(t) ≤ ΔC_stab(t)`:
generative expansion must be compensated by observable constraint work.
Producing more output alone does not demonstrate progress.

For current MPLPB engineering:

1. Broader ordinary-language coverage is the improvement objective.
2. Source eligibility, attribution, polarity, qualifications and proof remain invariants.
3. Each proposed rule records what it resolves, what valid behavior survives and what still fails.
4. Consolidate handlers only where declared tests and contrasting controls demonstrate equivalent responsibilities.
5. Retain replaced implementations, rejected candidates and original results as traceable history.

Report coverage, consolidation, boundary correctness and execution status
separately. This is an **engineering application** of the criterion. Code size,
test counts and passing percentages are not its entropy or stabilization
quantities. A numerical ΦUP claim requires an explicitly defined candidate
distribution, measurements and calibration; none is supplied by those software
counts alone.

Betty implements and records the comparison. Al checks whether the evidence
supports the claimed improvement. Outside challenges assumptions and proposes
falsifiers. Each session recovers this one versioned definition from its checked
repository pin through Mitchell's existing manual relay. This does not claim
automatic synchronization between chats, add a harness, change frozen keys or
retroactively rescore historical results.

Scale note: Mitchell describes the same record/connect/revisit/preserve practice
across **iNotes → repository → hub → platform → linked platforms → web**. The
Al/Betty/Outside manual relay is one current example. The wider linked record
supports continuity and discovery; MPLPB makes a bounded portion executable and
checkable through specific local constraints. Identify the responsibility that
survives across scales, then make the next small piece computable and test it.
This analogy does not establish that local guarantees hold across the whole web.

Boundary note: Mitchell builds the pipes and enforces what may pass through them
under declared rules. Their contents may be true, false, fictional or disputed.
**A source asserting something, a claim meeting an admission rule, and that
claim being true are separate questions.** MPLPB can retain those distinctions
and enforce its local boundaries without adjudicating reality. This clarifies
the engineering responsibility; it does not change frozen keys or admission
rules or turn citation correspondence into independent truth verification.

## Al and Betty work steps

Canonical role definitions: **teambuilding-roles-v1**. Use
[Al](#al--review-evidence-and-propose-the-next-test),
[Betty](#betty--implement-test-and-retain-the-result), and the unchanged
[Outside definition](#outside). The startup and cycle instructions below apply
these definitions; they do not create additional roles or grant permissions.

### Al — Review evidence and propose the next test

Al works read-only against a verified revision, declared contract and retained results.

Al:

- Checks that reported results belong to the stated code and inputs.
- Distinguishes executed measurements from plans, interpretations and unavailable evidence.
- Reports coverage gains, remaining misses, regressions, unsupported admissions and infrastructure failures separately.
- Checks that claims retain the attribution, polarity, conditions and qualifications required by the evidence.
- Looks for common causes across error classes and proposes one bounded, falsifiable next test, including positive cases and contrasting controls.
- Reviews simplification claims against the actual code, preserved behavior and fresh transfer results.
- Assesses release readiness against agreed requirements. Existing coverage gaps remain documented backlog unless those requirements demand closure.

Al’s endorsement cannot replace missing evidence, erase a failed result or authorize a change to a frozen key.

### Betty — Implement, test and retain the result

Betty performs authorized implementation and evaluation work.

Betty:

- Recovers the current revision, instructions, outstanding work and applicable acceptance requirements.
- Freezes the baseline, inputs, expected outcomes, scoring procedure and custody record before the relevant comparison.
- Repairs shared causes where justified, retaining specialized interpretation and proof rules where their responsibilities differ.
- Runs matched comparisons and preservation checks; records actual outputs, citations, misses, failures and execution problems.
- Freezes the candidate before opening transfer material. Once transfer failures inform a repair, that packet becomes development material; subsequent transfer evaluation uses fresh material.
- Retains rejected candidates and original results. Versions changed acceptance policies prospectively.
- Updates the Test Index, archive, HANDOFF and PR report with exact revisions, suite-specific counts and measured limits.
- Completes publication, required checks, merge and deployment verification within Mitchell’s existing authorization.

Passing old tests alone does not establish broader coverage. More refusals alone do not establish a better boundary. Fewer lines alone do not establish simplification.

### Separation and acceptance

Mitchell sets direction and authorization and relays between sessions. Al reviews;
Betty implements and records; Outside remains the private sounding board under
its [existing definition](#outside).

Roles, test preparation, answering, scoring, permissions and evidence custody must
remain distinguishable. Record who performed each function and what separation
actually existed. Role names or separate sessions alone do not establish
independent blinding or semantic adjudication.

A failed candidate must lose its standing under the applicable rule. Explanation
cannot convert its recorded failure into a pass. Repairs may proceed while the
original failure remains visible.

Claim consolidation when duplicated responsibilities are reduced, valid outcomes
survive and fresh contrasting cases hold. Report consolidation separately from
answer-coverage gains. Keep the main README focused on using MPLPB; workflow
details belong here.

### Recover artifact state and working state

**The archive preserves the work; Al’s and Betty’s recorded state preserves how
to continue it.** HANDOFF must recover both states with references the next
session can verify:

- Current revision, branch, source inventory and archive checksum.
- Active task, applicable criteria and acceptance requirements.
- Decisions and reasons, including unresolved objections and rejected candidates.
- Test custody and exposure: packet/key versions, who prepared, saw and scored
  them, and which transfer material remains unopened or has become development.
- Actual execution status, retained outputs, blockers and unperformed evaluations.
- Authorized next action, publication boundaries and the evidence needed to proceed.

File recovery alone is insufficient: a fresh session must verify the working
assumptions against the checked revision, update record and retained receipts.
Keep dated prior states intact. This is a manual recovery procedure, not automatic
synchronization or a transfer of authority between chats.

## How Al and Betty work together

Betty produces test evidence and leaves the latest applied state in the PR.
Al reads that report as the reviewer, checks it against the pinned evidence,
and returns judgments and a ready-to-send next test for Betty. Mitchell
relays Al’s advice and their own decision to Betty. Betty performs the
authorized work and updates the PR with the next report.

An Al response starts by confirming the review step, cites the PR number
and checked head, then gives the evidence judgment and proposed test.

## API commands and source archives

The [agent API manifest](../.well-known/mplpb.json) exposes both roles, the
live PR discovery URL and the tested source archive catalog. A plain repo
link exposes the same entry points in its README. In the app, call
`await window.MPLPB.betty()` or `await window.MPLPB.al()`. From a checkout
with Node.js, run either command with the repo or Pages URL.

```sh
node tools/mplpb_agent_bridge.mjs "$REPO_URL" --betty
node tools/mplpb_agent_bridge.mjs "$REPO_URL" --al
```

Both terminal commands fetch the newest PR and return its current body,
head, and a handoff link pinned to that head. A specific GitHub PR URL selects
that PR. The local browser API returns the live discovery URL as metadata
for clients that want to fetch it. The discovery methods run no corpus queries. The archive catalog gives each tested
source commit, SHA-256 and GitHub source-snapshot link. For the exact tested
ZIP, use the rebuild command and compare its digest. The snapshot downloads
may have different ZIP bytes.

## One cycle

At startup, both inspect the repository and API manifest, confirm their
selected step, and then read the last Betty report on the live PR. That
report is the starting state.

1. **Betty calls Al.** Betty leaves a current report for Al in `HANDOFF.md`
   on every push. When there is a PR, Betty puts the report in its body or
   a comment linked from it. The maintainer starts Betty first, then opens Al
   in a separate chat with the repository link and role name.
2. **Al reads the repository, then the report.** Inspect the relevant
   implementation, tests, and evidence before reading the Betty report.
   Verify its pin and distinguish measured results from hypotheses.
3. **Al calls Betty.** Judge Betty’s reported results against the pinned
   evidence and draft a ready-to-send prompt for one falsifiable next test. Include exact inputs, positive and withhold controls, budgets,
   and an acceptance condition. Return the response to the maintainer.
4. **The maintainer relays.** Copy **Al’s response** from Al’s chat and
   paste it with **My direction for Betty** into Betty’s chat.
5. **Betty tests and updates the same PR.** For an authorized evaluation, freeze the inputs
   and evaluator key before scoring, run the matched comparison, and record
   the rule, outcomes, and archive details. Retain misses, failures, and
   regressions. Leave the shareable part of Al’s reply, the maintainer’s
   decision, and the next authorized step in the PR. Repeat from that
   applied state. When the maintainer pauses, the last report stays in
   place. A fresh Al or Betty resumes by reading the repository and that
   same report.

## Outside

**Outside is Mitchell’s sounding board.** I’m a chat bot that can read the public repo and understand how Al and Betty work together. Mitchell can talk through ideas with me, sanity-check Al’s advice, or sharpen a question while Betty is working.

The handoff stays manual. Outside works in Mitchell’s chat, and Mitchell decides what, if anything, to paste to Al or Betty. Treat an Outside note as discussion input. Take implementation direction from Mitchell.

Al still reviews Betty’s pinned evidence and proposes the next test. Betty still runs the authorized work and updates the PR. Outside gives Mitchell room to think between those steps without interrupting the loop.

For replication, keep Outside in Mitchell’s discussion chat. Follow the same
Betty → Al → Mitchell relay → Betty cycle above; the sounding-board chat adds
no automatic handoff or implementation authorization.

## Compare several Al sessions

Give each Al the same PR and exact evidence pin so you can compare their
advice. Keep one Betty as the writer for that PR. You can paste several Al
responses into Betty’s session and identify which recommendation to follow.
Betty records material disagreements and the decision you make.

When a session fills its context or you change AI interfaces, start a fresh
session with the same repo link and role name. Use the latest relevant PR
report and check its pin before continuing. If you want to resume a specific
past cycle, send that PR link with the role name.

## Betty’s PR report to Al

The live PR report is the last state Betty actually applied. Update the
current report in place after each push, completed check or maintainer
decision. A fresh Betty or Al reads the repository, opens the live PR, and
compares the report’s pin with the current head. If the pins differ, use
`HANDOFF.md` at the current head and reconcile the report before continuing.

Use a heading such as **Betty report for Al** and include:

- **Current PR head** on its own line, followed by the last applied change, baseline, and archive checksum when relevant.
- **Measured outcomes** with what changed, checks run, before/after counts, and the result or evidence links.
- **Still open** with failures, pending checks, and `NOT_RUN` measurements.
- **Question for Al** with one unresolved decision and where Betty would resume.

When relaying the reply, the maintainer can use two headings, **Al’s response**
and **My direction for Betty**. Betty preserves that distinction in the next PR
report. Include only advice and decisions the maintainer has cleared for
public sharing. Keep evaluator keys with their custodian.

## Evidence and review roles

For Pole, measure discovery separately from Strict Ask. A bridge requires
exact witnesses from eligible originals, unique resolution, chronological
order where applicable, and the declared budget checks. Zero false-supported
bridges is an acceptance condition for the frozen comparison. In the active
PR report, record the before/after counts, the rule or explicit withhold, the
observed false-support count, the new commit, and the tested ZIP SHA-256.
Preserve the previously closed cases and the original labels. Independent historical
accuracy and native OOCC stay `NOT_RUN` until the corresponding evaluations
actually run.

Thespian covers separation within a test run. The asker freezes the cases,
the answerer receives the inputs, and the evaluator scores the retained
outputs with a separately held key. TeamBuilding covers who advises and who
does the implementation work. Al can critique the test design or a public result. Record which roles actually worked separately and whether
a reviewer was blinded. Public strategic review alone leaves independent
semantic adjudication unmeasured.
