# Closing historical-answer gaps without promoting guesses

Last reviewed: 2026-11-18.

The frozen22 development questions are not a22-answer accuracy target. Some demand ungrounded causality, comprehensive responsibility or historical priority. Keep complete source-supported answers, checked partials, conflicts, refusals and unknown semantic correctness separate.

`tools/report_fixpack_retest.py` compares fresh direct workers under the unchanged source/question seals. It uses draft references only after execution, retains every missed anchor with original-byte mapping, compares native claims/citations/missing obligations and reports CPU/wall/RSS/logical reads. Draft span coverage is not answerability or historical recall.

## Separate exploration budget

```bash
python -m mplpb_work.answer_router explore 'YOUR COMPLETE QUESTION' --ledger LEDGER --max-passages 12
```

Discovery searches up to four question parts separately, preserves a contrary-wording inspection slot when found, and reports unshown contrary candidates and truncation. Native paragraph indexing is capped at2048 paragraphs/1,000,000 characters. It never calls strict answering with shortened questions or source hints, never consumes native proof slots and never sets answer support. `ask` remains strict-first.

## Independently checked local context

Kernel v5 can retain an explicit action with a referential object and the complete local paragraph as a checked partial. Topic words in that paragraph do not resolve the referent. “Mira surveyed the area” near “Cedar Valley” cannot become “Mira surveyed Cedar Valley.” The independent checker rereads the eligible body, derives the action and paragraph witness, preserves uncertainty, and keeps the question obligation unresolved. Conditions, quotes, source changes and cross-paragraph bridges do not gain support.

The U06 development pilot retains “Ferdinand Hayden surveyed the area in1871” with the unresolved-area qualification. That is a partial-source improvement, not a complete answer, resolved identity or independent historical truth. Full frozen comparison is required before reporting a corpus-wide gain.

## Assessment and further proof work

Freeze the case table, questions, outputs and original source bytes. Give a fresh AI session only those materials, with prior scores, expected answers and implementation changes withheld. Require exact supporting quotes or an explicit unreviewed/uncertain verdict. Label it AI assessment, not independent human validation. Keep unknown human times and historical precision/recall null.

Use the [flag-only triage sidecar](SEMANTIC_TRIAGE.md) for unresolved review pairs and [generation publishing](LEDGER_GENERATIONS.md) for crash-aware writes. Neither adds historical proof. New rules must preserve every necessary connection and retain negation, reported/intended actions and date roles. Similar wording cannot merge events, a document date cannot become an event date, and chronology cannot create causality.

Exploration now traverses eligible sources round-robin and reuses the native1200-character wrapped-line context units, preserving exact canonical slices and incomplete-context labels. This prevents an early long source from consuming the whole discovery index. It changes no strict proof slots or grammar.
