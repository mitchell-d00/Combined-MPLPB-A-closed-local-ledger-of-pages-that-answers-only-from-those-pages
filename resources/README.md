This is a curated encyclopedia selection, not an entire Wikipedia download. History of computing hardware was requested but unavailable under that exact Simple English title; no replacement page was invented. Computer and Computer science cover computing in the included set. No image files were fetched.

`manifest.json` pins resource bytes, acquisition dates and upstream identities. Source hashes establish byte consistency, not accuracy or authenticated authorship. Wikipedia authorship is unknown, independence is unverified, and captures are historical observations, not live scores. Older evaluation captures and frozen probes were not changed.

In chat: `define dog`, `synonyms dog`, `what does fossil mean`, `language resources`. Choose **Reference encyclopedia** in the corpus menu, then `show my MPLPB` to explore its pages. Lookups are explicit dictionary operations; synset membership does not establish contextual interchangeability. Synonyms do not modify ledger ownership, infer new topic facts or select a page automatically. Grammar data is a developer reference, not an enabled syntax parser.

The browser bundles these offline resources, increasing its initial download and memory use. Deleting user collections does not delete bundled references. Remove/revert this resource pack to remove bundled data. Revert its module, corpus registration and runtime inclusion together; preserve unrelated captures and chats.
