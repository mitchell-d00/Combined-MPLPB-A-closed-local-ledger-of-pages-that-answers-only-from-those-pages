Photosynthesis

Photosynthesis is a process in which green plants make their own food from sunlight, using the help of leaves to produce sugar. It is the process by which these organisms convert light energy into chemical energy, which is used to produce food. Photosynthesis is the process by which plant cells containing chlorophyll produce food substances (glucose and starch) from carbon dioxide and water, and oxygen is released.
