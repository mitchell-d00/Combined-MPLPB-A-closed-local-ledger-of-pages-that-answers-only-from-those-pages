# Frozen attribution trial rule

Frozen before implementation. Baseline: PR #101 head
`28b8feb871cd0c2eb29440f2ab46623e410622b6`.

The failure class is binding an instruction to its stated speaker while
preserving the source's reporting layer. A printed letter signature binds its
first-person voice; a named instructor in minutes or a quoted report remains
distinct from the recorder, writer and reporter. Quoted instructions can support
what the source attributes to the explicit speaker. They cannot establish
authenticated authorship, occurrence or execution. No instruction proves that
the action was completed.

Development: three source forms × four instruction constructions × four actor
or completion variants = 48 cases. The different-quoted-speaker variant asks
about the actual attributed speaker, so it is positive. The wrong-actor and
execution questions withhold. Expected counts follow evidence: 24 positive and
24 withholding decisions, superseding the initial proposed 12/36 split before
any expectation or runtime freeze. Transfer: three forms × two structurally
different constructions × the same four variants = 24 cases, 12 positive and
12 withholding decisions. Transfer includes passive/recipient-first and
postposed quotation attribution, with disjoint actors, tasks and question forms.

The generator and seed are public reproducibility inputs. The same operator
authored the generator, saw its templates, froze and scored both sets. Exact
transfer case bytes and key stay unopened until the committed candidate and
source ZIP are frozen. Thus this is **same-operator transfer testing**, not
independent preparation or blinding. Unopened bytes do not make visible
generator templates unseen. Historical accuracy, independent preparation,
independent blinding/adjudication, outside-custodian scoring and native OOCC
remain NOT_RUN.

Freeze generator bytes, seed, input hashes, rule, harness and separate scoring
keys before runtime edits. Retain baseline native outputs. The answer worker
receives inputs and implementation only. The scorer checks actor, writer,
recipient, task, source voice, affirmative instruction-only modality, no
completion claim, and exact canonical citations. A negative with any supported
claim fails; a positive with a wrong role or witness fails as false support.
Unknown forms and exhausted budgets are misses or budget failures, never passes.

Run a committed frozen candidate on transfer once. Retain every failure. Do not
repair based on that transfer packet and continue calling it transfer. Any such
repair requires a fresh frozen transfer set for a new transfer claim.

Preserve all 17 PE-02 cases and 28 targeted regressions. Compare complete answer
contracts (excluding code fingerprints), including meaning, attribution,
qualifications, missing obligations and citations, plus discovery outputs.
Preserve withdrawal, ambiguity, mutation, derived-source, context and budget
controls. Keep limits of eight records, 200,000 scanned characters, 1,000 units,
twelve passages, 8,000 output characters, and existing phrase bounds. The native
checker independently rereads currently eligible bytes and checks final state.

Acceptance requires every frozen decision and field/witness check to pass,
positive coverage in each source form, zero false-supported claims and all
preservation checks. Failed acceptance remains failed. Publish development and
transfer independently, retain all rejected candidates, archive the committed
source and test its extraction before publication.
