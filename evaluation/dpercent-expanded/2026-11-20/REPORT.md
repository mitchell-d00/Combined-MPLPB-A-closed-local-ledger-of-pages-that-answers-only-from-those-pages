# Expanded D% known-error stress test

Last reviewed: 2026-11-20.

A new generator combines the unchanged 31 original D% historical-prose families
with 17 literal-field access and boundary families. It reuses the existing D%
isolated runner and worker; neither generator nor key is mounted into execution.
Runtime source packages remain identical to the tested field-normalization pin
044ac27. No engine rules are changed during this generated batch.

The field charts vary ten question structures, three field vocabularies, LF/CRLF,
Unicode bodies, repeated/different identifiers, changed bodies, quoted/reported
headers, source restrictions, withdrawal and corruption. A conservative 1,440
field structural cells are possible before name expansion; the original prose
families additionally retain wrapping, layout, tense, modality, correction,
identity, causal, missing-link and date-role variants. Charts do not discard
questions that the engine cannot parse. Synonyms and compound requests remain
positive tests even when the current checker cannot answer them.

A 1,000-case batch is sealed before execution. Both answer and refusal labels
are in a disjoint custody directory. Every d100 draw and rejected sampling draw
is retained there; public seals commit to keys, dice, generator and worker hashes.
Source packets contain no expected outcomes. Five generator/scorer tests passed,
including deterministic replay, tamper detection, key separation, structural
variation and rejection of wrong field values or invalid quotation anchors.

Results are pending at this freeze; no full-batch pass, precision or coverage gain
is asserted. Cases that require unsupported semantic scoring remain unscored.
This is known-error developer-authored stress testing, not independent/unseen
validation or proof of historical truth. A generated expected answer is a stated
synthetic test obligation, not independent evidence of real historical semantics.

The measurement scorer reuses the original quote/hash verification, then admits
field answers only when the complete required field set matches exactly.
Conflicts, missing links and unknown evidence cannot be silently scored as answers.
Counts, denominators, E (correct complete answer detection), F (false support on
refusal cases), errors, unscored outputs and costs are reported separately.
