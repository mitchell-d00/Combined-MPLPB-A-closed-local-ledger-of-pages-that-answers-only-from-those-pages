Record operating system, prior experience, failures and any coaching. No live reader or collection upgrades during the trial.

Score support, missing evidence, conflict and instruction-without-execution separately. A source-scoped instruction is not completed execution. Preserve failed setup, blocked launches, no output and unanswered tasks separately from abstentions. Never replace missing participants with simulated users.
