Recruit first-time users by permission through Mitchell. Screen and record
previous exposure. Assign a participant code and provide
[the tasks](START_HERE.md), a clean browser profile, and a blank
[return form](return.json). Do not explain the answer, citation labels or
what the user should conclude. Stop each task after five minutes, on
participant withdrawal or on completion. Record assistance separately.
Do not ask participants to share private documents or personally identifiable
data. Keep recordings or identifying information outside the evidence
repository unless separate consent covers publication.

For the three tasks score independently whether the participant found the
right passage, accurately explained the difference between a refusal and
evidence of absence, and created and cited the new eligible source. Record
elapsed time, context, navigation, failure stage and any assistance for each.
Software and pilot dry runs are separate. Publish aggregate data only after
consent, cohort and exact browser state are preserved. No participant is
recruited or scored by this checkpoint.
