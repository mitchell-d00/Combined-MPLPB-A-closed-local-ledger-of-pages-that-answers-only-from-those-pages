# Signed-letter instruction rule frozen before implementation

Last reviewed: 2026-11-25.

General failure: discovery of a directive does not bind its speaker or addressee.
Accept a source-attributed instruction only in one uniquely matching eligible
original letter with an explicit salutation, a complete full-name signature,
an unqualified first-person instructions declaration, and the complete stated
task of the requested delegation. The requested actor must match that full
signature (or its unique surname), and the requested recipient must match the
salutation. A loose mention, publisher heading, question premise or initials
alone cannot establish this binding.

The finite class accepts task questions about a named actor and delegation in
a letter to a named recipient. A two-obligation transition/task question may
retain only the instruction proof; preserve its exact unresolved first question
in missing obligations. Never promote that partial to completion or infer an
agreement, successful negotiation, transmission, acceptance or execution.

The statement form is a complete source unit stating "The task of ... is to ...".
If it refers to "these objectives", retain the immediately governing explicit
objectives as source witnesses. Preserve the entire task, its quantifiers and
prospective modality. Verify all witnesses independently from eligible current
canonical body bytes. Quote, negation, conditional/example scope, wrong or
ambiguous authorship, competing instructions, derived pages and missing sources
withhold. Unrelated quotation elsewhere must not itself create a witness.

Keep eight records, 200,000 scanned characters, 1,000 units, twelve passages,
8,000 output characters and the existing phrase limits. Keep eligibility,
independent original-byte checking and final source-state verification. Add
source-state, ambiguity, qualification and budget regression controls.
