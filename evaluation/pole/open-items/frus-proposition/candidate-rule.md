original. Find the unique complete native source unit enclosing that span.
Ask the existing native assertion verifier to check that entire unit as
an action statement and a relationship statement under the unchanged
question and internal profile. Run a single check per comparison with at

and preserve the source's exact words, speaker and reporting layers.

when existing extraction supplies a complete heading and enclosing witness.

speaker, any intermediary reporter, and the relation and modality stated
in the document. A complete comparison needs both admissions on distinct
eligible originals with unique resolution. Any separately asserted

certificate binds the required proposition and attribution, withhold.

author's intent, an instruction, advice, or a forecast.
