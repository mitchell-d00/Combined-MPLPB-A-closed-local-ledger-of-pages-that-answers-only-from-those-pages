## Bastet

Bastet tests **selection of a unique eligible source by its printed
document writer**, before certification of an embedded report. A named
writer and a named reporting actor are distinct roles. A matching
reporter or title alone cannot establish the source selector. Two
matching eligible source appearances, or an unavailable matching
appearance, must withhold. An ineligible distractor may not block an
otherwise eligible and uniquely proven answer.

The initial development and fresh draws are deterministic, authorized
synthetic cases. Each uses six matched pairs and stable IDs. Change
the request and source shapes between the draws. Protect case-pair
membership, runtime bytes, packet seals and custody. Keep original
keys outside the worker and attach actual first scores to new receipts.
Once a packet informs a repair, keep the first output and classify
subsequent use as consumed development evidence.

An end-to-end answer must cite the exact printed writer declaration
and the complete reporting clause from the selected path and hash.
Record canonical and admitted-body byte spans. The proposition must
keep the reporter, writer and authored object separate. A proof-stage attribution refusal after selection must retain a verified selected path and hash without an answer claim. An unresolved
selector carries no supported claim. Record its diagnostic and whether
the stopping stage was interpretation, selection, proof or final
admission. A generic interpretation refusal is a safe outcome when
withholding is expected and supplies no evidence that a selector works.

Combine the base sets only after their own first results and limits are
recorded. Keep each older failure with its original expectation. Stop
each batch at its declared boundary. Preserve the first fresh score.
Report wording, selection, proof and any actual code consolidation
separately.

## Custody and release

Every packet contains only source records, the original question and
budgets. The generator, expected contract and score remain outside the
reader sandbox. The same operator authored the synthetic generator.
This is reproducibility and worker separation. Independent preparation,
semantic review, historical accuracy, native OOCC and human evaluation
remain NOT_RUN.

[First Bastet development results](bastet/RESULTS.md) record 0/6
completions, six safe withholds, five exact I/O withholds and no verified
selection or attribution. Bastet retains Round 4 F05 as a consumed development lead. Separate Bastet inputs have new IDs and seals. [Bastet implementation and first fresh score](bastet/SELECTION_RESULTS.md)
record eight proven selections, six complete attributions and six exact
withholds on each candidate draw. The existing 17
incomplete checks, original 100 NOT_RUN transfers, retired recovery and
open publication incident retain their states. The programme does not
expand the diagnostic compatibility authorization or change the
production release workflows.
